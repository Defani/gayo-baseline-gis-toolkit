qgisMinimumVersion=3.16
description=A QGIS plugin for the Gayo coffee farmer baseline survey (Aceh Tengah and Bener Meriah): pull KoboToolbox data, show survey photos at their points on the map, run quick QC queries, measure distance to forest areas, and export to Excel/GeoJSON/CSV/PostGIS.
version=0.11
author=Defani Arman (GIS Consultant, TFCA Sumatera Program - Rumah Indonesia Berkelanjutan and Redelong Institute)
email=defaniarman@gmail.com

about=A QGIS plugin built for the Gayo coffee farmer baseline assessment (Konsorsium Konservasi Kopi Gayo) in Aceh Tengah and Bener Meriah, Aceh. Author: Defani Arman, GIS Consultant, TFCA Sumatera Program, Rumah Indonesia Berkelanjutan and Redelong Institute. Tools, all opened from one sidebar panel: (1) KoboToolbox connection to pull survey submissions as a QGIS layer; (2) Foto di Layer - download survey photos and show them at their coordinates (map tip and attribute-form preview); (3) Foto Explorer for photos in Kobo repeat groups; (4) Quick Query for search, filter and QC presets; (5) Distance Analysis from survey points to the nearest forest area with its status; (6) export to Excel, GeoJSON, CSV or PostGIS/Supabase; (7) add web tiles (XYZ/WMTS/WMS/WFS), files of any format, or Google Earth Engine layers; (8) GPX export for Garmin; (9) Grid Index for map series and atlases. Third-party components: Grid Index adapted from the plugin by Kapildev Adhikari (GPLv3); GEE catalog trimmed from geemap by Qiusheng Wu (MIT).
